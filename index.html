Concentric

Pick a face, then add it to your Home Screen.

  • Panel Dial, date, calendar and progress
  • Clock Just the dial, edge to edge
  • Horizon A large hour over two arcs
  1. Open a face in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the Home Screen: it runs full screen, offline, and keeps the screen on.
  4. Tap the clock to switch faces.
